From interviews
to a backlog

I ran user interview calls, structured every piece of feedback into one place in Notion, and turned it into a prioritized backlog the team builds from.

Role
Product Designer — research & synthesis
Timeline
2025
Platform
Notion · Discovery
Focus
Interviews · Synthesis
Context

Feedback everywhere, structure nowhere

User feedback was coming in through calls, but it lived in scattered notes — hard to act on. To feed the roadmap, that raw input needed a system: captured, organized, and prioritized.

What I did

How I structured it

  • Ran interview calls with users and captured the conversations as I went.
  • Structured every insight into a single source of truth in Notion — themes, patterns, and requests.
  • Turned that synthesis into a prioritized backlog of new activities for the team.
Outcome

A living backlog grounded in real user feedback that the team pulls new work from.
