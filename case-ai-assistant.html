Context

Designed for how doctors actually work

Doctors move fast and can't afford friction. The goal was an assistant that fits into clinical workflows — answering, summarizing, and surfacing the right information without adding cognitive load. Because the product is still in beta, its name and detailed screens are kept private here.

What I did

From interaction model to screens

  • Shaped the core interaction patterns — how the assistant asks, responds, and hands control back to the doctor.
  • Designed the conversation states end to end, from empty state to results and follow-ups.
  • Took it from concept to high-fidelity screens, ready for build.
Outcome

Now in beta with early clinical users, with a broader launch planned this year.
