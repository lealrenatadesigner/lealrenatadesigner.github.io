White-label,
self-serve

A client-side layer that lets customers brand the whole product themselves — typography, colors, identity — with no engineering involvement. Owned end to end: concept, design, and testing.

Role
Product Designer — sole owner
Timeline
2025
Platform
Web · B2B SaaS
Focus
Theming · Design tokens
Context

Every branding change was a tech ticket

Clients wanted the product to look like theirs, but every color or font change meant involving engineering. That's slow for the client and a drain on the tech team. The opportunity: let clients own their own look, safely.

What I did

What I did

  • Designed the whole personalization model — turning design-system tokens into controls a non-technical client can use.
  • Kept it safe by design, so client choices stay within the system's guardrails.
  • Tested it thoroughly to make sure real users could style the product on their own.
Outcome

Clients now self-serve their entire brand — typography, colors, identity — with the tech team out of the loop for styling.
